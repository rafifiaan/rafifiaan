## 🚀 About Me

**Fresh Graduate in Informatics** from **Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)** 🎓

I started my career in **Software Engineer**—focusing on building backend systems and APIs, which gave me a strong understanding of system behavior and data flow. This foundation led me to QA Automation, where I focus on ensuring software quality through automated testing, including Regression, UAT, API, and Visual Testing, to deliver stable and reliable releases. I am well-versed in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC), and experienced in Agile environments, collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality software efficiently. I am currently growing my career toward an SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) role.
